# Icon credits

Logos belong to their owners and are used only to identify the link they open.

Logos added in v0.6.0 come from each site's own app icon or favicon, Google's
favicon service, or high-resolution copies found through Google Images:
boces (Eastern Suffolk BOCES), canva, citation (Zotero), cuny, edgenuity, fafsa
(Federal Student Aid), geogebra, gutenberg (Wikimedia Commons), libraryCatalog
(Follett), newsbank, phet, photopea, regents (NYSED), scratch, sora, wayground,
writingLab (Purdue).

Links with no usable logo (apRegistration, nysAid, programOfStudies,
university/SUNY) use [Material Symbols](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons)
(Rounded, filled, weight 700) by Google, Apache License 2.0, as 512×512 PNGs.
